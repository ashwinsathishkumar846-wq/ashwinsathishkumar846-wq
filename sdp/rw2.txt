177|To understand how permissions work in JIRA.
178|Permissions are settings in Jira applications that decide what users can view and do inside them. Jira offers many kinds of permissions, from the ability to create new projects to the ability to see a particular kind of comment on an issue, and they may vary from one application to another.
179|Jira applications have three types of permissions, going from broad to detailed:
180|Global permissions – Cover the whole application rather than single projects (for example, whether users can see other users).
181|Project permissions – Grouped into permission schemes and applied to projects (for example, who can view, create, edit and assign the issues of a project).
182|Issue security permissions – Grouped into security schemes; they let the visibility of single issues be adjusted within the limits of the project's permissions.
